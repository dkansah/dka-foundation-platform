# Campaign Manager Documentation

Comprehensive 6-week rollout calendar and multi-channel campaign strategy for DKA FOUNDATION launch.

## 📋 Contents

### 1. **rollout-calendar.json**
Week-by-week campaign structure:
- **Week 1:** Launch & Awareness
- **Week 2:** Impact Stories
- **Week 3:** Values & Engagement
- **Week 4:** Call to Action
- **Week 5:** Momentum & Partnerships
- **Week 6:** Closing Push

Includes specific activities by day, channel, content type, and performance targets.

### 2. **social-posts.json**
10 ready-to-use social media posts covering:
- Platform-specific messaging
- Hashtag strategies
- Call-to-action buttons
- Visual & video descriptions
- Engagement targets

### 3. **email-templates.md**
8 fully customizable email campaigns:
1. Welcome Email (New Donor)
2. Monthly Impact Report
3. Volunteer Opportunity
4. Event Invitation
5. Win-Back Campaign (Lapsed Donor)
6. Partnership Inquiry
7. Seasonal Campaign (Holiday Giving)
8. Urgent Appeal (Crisis Relief)

### 4. **persona-matrix.json**
5 Donor Personas with:
- Best channels for reaching them
- Messaging tone & triggers
- Ideal content types
- Sample subject lines & copy

## 🎯 Pro Tips

1. **Consistency is Key** — Post on schedule, always
2. **Authenticity Wins** — Real stories > Polished marketing
3. **Ask for Help** — Encourage user-generated content & testimonials
4. **Respond Quickly** — Engage with comments within 2 hours
5. **Test & Iterate** — Try different messaging, measure what works
6. **Celebrate Wins** — Share successes to build momentum
7. **Thank Donors** — Personal gratitude creates loyalty
8. **Track Everything** — What gets measured gets improved
