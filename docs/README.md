# DKA FOUNDATION — Legal Documents

This directory contains all legally-required governance and planning documents for DKA FOUNDATION.

## 📄 Documents Included

### 1. **Constitution** (`constitution.md`)
The foundational governing document setting out:
- Charity name and objects (purposes)
- Trustee duties and requirements
- Membership structure
- Meeting procedures
- Financial management
- Dissolution clause

**Status:** Ready for Charity Commission submission

### 2. **Mission, Vision & Values** (`mission-vision-values.md`)
Inspirational and strategic framework including:
- Public mission statement
- Long-term vision
- Seven core values (Innovation, Compassion, Integrity, Collaboration, Sustainability, Equity, Resilience)
- How values are lived in practice
- Strategic focus areas
- Impact goals through 2030

**Use:** Website, branding, donor communications, annual reports

### 3. **Charitable Objects Statement** (`charitable-objects.md`)
Legal description of charitable purposes aligned with the 13 UK categories:
- Advancement of education
- Advancement of health
- Community development
- Arts, culture, heritage, science
- Amateur sport
- Human rights & reconciliation
- Environmental protection
- Relief of those in need
- Animal welfare
- Public service efficiency
- Advancement of religion
- Other charitable purposes

**Use:** Charity Commission application form (Section 1)

### 4. **Trustee Forms** (`trustee-forms.md`)
Comprehensive trustee documentation:
- **Form 1:** Trustee Declaration (eligibility & duties)
- **Form 2:** Trustee Information Form (background & experience)
- **Form 3:** Financial Information Form (for Treasurer)
- **Form 4:** Disqualification Check Declaration
- Trustee onboarding checklist

**Use:** Trustee recruitment, Charity Commission application

### 5. **Financial Plan** (`financial-plan.md`)
Year 1 financial projections including:
- Income sources & growth targets (Year 1-5)
- Expenditure by category
- Budget allocation by impact area
- Cash flow projections
- Break-even analysis
- Risk mitigation strategies
- Financial controls
- KPIs and targets

**Use:** Charity Commission application, donor pitches, strategic planning

## ✅ Submission Checklist for Charity Commission

When applying to register as a charity with the Charity Commission for England & Wales:

- [ ] **Constitution** (this document, completed and signed)
- [ ] **Trustee Declaration Forms** (one per trustee, signed)
- [ ] **Charity Objects Statement** (copy from section 2.3)
- [ ] **Financial Plan** (Year 1 projections)
- [ ] **Application Form** (completed online via Charity Commission portal)
- [ ] **Proof of Trustees' Identity** (passport/driving license copies)
- [ ] **Proof of Address** (utility bill, council tax letter)
- [ ] **Conflict of Interest Register** (initial version)

**Typical Processing Time:** 40–60 days from complete submission

**Charity Registration Cost:** Free

## 📋 How to Use These Documents

### For Trustees
1. Read the Constitution carefully
2. Sign the Trustee Declaration Forms
3. Complete the Trustee Information Form
4. Review Mission/Vision/Values
5. Familiarize yourself with Financial Plan

### For Donors & Partners
1. Read the Mission, Vision & Values
2. Review the Charitable Objects Statement to understand what the charity does
3. Check the Financial Plan to see how funds are allocated
4. Review annual reports (to be published once registered)

### For Public Communications
1. Use Mission Statement on website and materials
2. Communicate Vision to inspire supporters
3. Showcase Values in annual reports and impact stories
4. Feature Charitable Objects in funding applications

### For Regulatory Compliance
1. Submit Constitution to Charity Commission (registration)
2. File Trustee Forms with Charity Commission
3. Maintain Financial Plan and update quarterly
4. Report annually to Charity Commission using accounts & narrative
5. Update Constitution if governance changes (with Commission approval)
