# Charitable Objects Statement — DKA FOUNDATION

## Legal Charitable Objects

**The objects of DKA FOUNDATION are for the public benefit:**

The advancement of education, including innovation, science, and technology; the advancement of health and the saving of lives; the advancement of citizenship and community development; the advancement of the arts, culture, heritage, and science; the advancement of amateur sport; the advancement of human rights, conflict resolution, and reconciliation; the advancement of environmental protection and improvement; the relief of those in need by reason of youth, age, ill-health, disability, financial hardship, or other disadvantage; the advancement of animal welfare; the promotion of the efficiency of the armed forces, police, fire, and rescue services; the advancement of religion; and any other purposes currently recognized as charitable under the laws of England and Wales.

## Public Benefit Test

DKA FOUNDATION meets the Charity Commission's public benefit test:

### Identifiable Benefit
✅ Programs deliver tangible benefits to individuals and communities
✅ Services address real needs (education, health, environment, rights)
✅ Beneficiaries can be identified (youth, patients, local communities)

### Appropriate and Proportionate
✅ Benefits appropriate to stated charitable purposes
✅ Resources proportionately allocated to maximize impact
✅ No unreasonable restrictions on beneficiaries

### Public Rather Than Private
✅ Benefits available to broad sections of community
✅ No private individuals receive improper financial benefit
✅ Programs open and accessible to eligible beneficiaries
✅ Governance ensures public accountability

### Detriment
✅ No identifiable detriment to the public
✅ Programs do not harm vulnerable groups
✅ Environmental initiatives support sustainability
