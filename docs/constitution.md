# CONSTITUTION OF DKA FOUNDATION

## 1. Name
The charity shall be called **DKA FOUNDATION**.

## 2. Objects
The charity is established for the following charitable purposes, recognized under UK law:

### 2.1 Advancement of Education
Including innovation, science, and technology training programs.

### 2.2 Advancement of Health or Saving Lives
Through medical outreach, wellness programs, and healthcare support.

### 2.3 Advancement of Citizenship or Community Development
Including social innovation hubs and community empowerment initiatives.

### 2.4 Advancement of Arts, Culture, Heritage, or Science
With emphasis on creative innovation and scientific research.

### 2.5 Advancement of Amateur Sport
Promoting youth participation and inclusion in sports.

### 2.6 Advancement of Human Rights, Conflict Resolution, or Reconciliation
Supporting peacebuilding and justice initiatives.

### 2.7 Advancement of Environmental Protection or Improvement
Including sustainable innovation projects and conservation.

### 2.8 Relief of Those in Need
Due to poverty, age, disability, or other disadvantage.

### 2.9 Advancement of Animal Welfare
Promoting humane treatment and conservation.

### 2.10 Promotion of Efficiency of Public Services
Supporting emergency response and safety services.

### 2.11 Advancement of Religion
Respecting diverse faith traditions and beliefs.

### 2.12 Other Charitable Purposes
Any other charitable purposes recognized by law, including innovation for public benefit.

## 3. Powers
To further the objects, the charity may:

- Raise funds and accept donations
- Acquire property and equipment
- Employ staff and volunteers
- Cooperate with other charities, authorities, and organizations
- Invest funds as permitted by law
- Provide grants and support to beneficiaries

## 4. Trustees

### 4.1 Composition
The charity shall have a minimum of three trustees and a maximum as determined by the trustees.

### 4.2 Duties
Trustees must:
- Act in the best interests of the charity
- Comply with Charity Commission guidance
- Maintain confidentiality
- Avoid conflicts of interest
- Exercise due diligence in financial matters

### 4.3 Restrictions
Trustees may not:
- Personally profit from charity funds
- Receive payment except for reasonable expenses
- Use charity assets for personal benefit
- Act while disqualified under the Charities Act

### 4.4 Terms
Trustees serve renewable terms of three years.

## 5. Membership

### 5.1 Eligibility
Membership is open to individuals and organizations supporting the charity's objects.

### 5.2 Rights
Members have voting rights at the Annual General Meeting (AGM).

### 5.3 Membership Fees
Membership fees (if any) shall be determined by trustees.

## 6. Meetings

### 6.1 Trustee Meetings
Trustees shall meet at least quarterly to conduct business.

### 6.2 Annual General Meeting
An AGM shall be held annually within 4 months of the financial year-end to:
- Review accounts and activities
- Elect or re-elect trustees
- Approve budget for next year
- Discuss strategic direction

### 6.3 Decision Making
- Decisions made by majority vote
- Chair has casting vote in case of tie
- Quorum: majority of trustees present

### 6.4 Notice
Members shall receive at least 14 days' notice of AGM.

## 7. Finance

### 7.1 Application of Income
All income and property shall be applied solely towards the charity's objects.

### 7.2 Trustee Expenses
Trustees may be reimbursed for reasonable expenses incurred in charity business.

### 7.3 Surplus Funds
Surplus funds must be reinvested into charitable activities, not distributed to members.

### 7.4 Banking
All funds held in bank accounts in the charity's name. Cheques require two signatories.

### 7.5 Audit
Annual financial audit conducted as required by Charity Commission.

## 8. Accounts & Reporting

### 8.1 Annual Accounts
Annual accounts shall be prepared in accordance with UK charity law.

### 8.2 Submission
Accounts submitted to Charity Commission within 10 months of year-end.

### 8.3 Transparency
Transparency and accountability maintained at all times through:
- Annual reporting
- Public financial statements
- Impact reports
- Beneficiary testimonials

### 8.4 Record Keeping
Trustee records, meeting minutes, and financial documents retained for 7 years.

## 9. Dissolution

### 9.1 Procedure
If the charity is dissolved, remaining assets must be transferred to another charity with similar purposes as approved by Charity Commission.

### 9.2 No Personal Benefit
No assets distributed to members or trustees on dissolution.

## 10. Amendment
This Constitution may be amended by resolution of trustees, subject to Charity Commission approval where required.
