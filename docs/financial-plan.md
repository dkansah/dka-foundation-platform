## Financial Sustainability Strategy

### 1. Diversified Income Streams
- Individual donors (recurring gifts)
- Grant funding (foundation & government)
- Corporate partnerships (cause-related marketing)
- Earned income (training courses, consulting)
- Fundraising events (galas, auctions)
- Online campaigns (crowdfunding)

### 2. Donor Retention
- Target retention rate: 85%+
- Personalized thank-you communications
- Impact reporting (quarterly updates)
- Exclusive donor events
- Upgrade path from donor → advocate

### 3. Cost Management
- Lean staffing model (part-time/volunteer)
- Shared office space or home-based initially
- Outsource non-core functions
- Negotiate vendor discounts
- Use free/low-cost tools (Google Workspace, Canva)

### 4. Revenue Growth Targets
- Year 1: £11,000 (launch phase)
- Year 2: £18,000 (+64%)
- Year 3: £28,000 (+56%)
- Year 5: £60,000+ (sustainable organization)

## Financial Controls

### Banking
- Separate charity bank account (not personal)
- Two-signatory requirement for payments
- Monthly reconciliation
- Online banking with audit trail

### Budgeting
- Annual budget approved by trustees
- Quarterly variance analysis
- Monthly management accounts
- Donor-restricted fund tracking

### Reporting
- Annual accounts to Charity Commission
- Quarterly reports to trustees
- Annual impact report to donors
- Tax compliance (Gift Aid, payroll if applicable)

### Audit
- Independent financial audit (annual)
- Charity Commission returns (annual)
- Internal controls review (quarterly)
- Beneficiary feedback tracking (ongoing)
