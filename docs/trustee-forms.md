## Checklist for Trustee Onboarding

When a trustee joins DKA FOUNDATION, ensure completion of:

✅ Form 1: Trustee Declaration

✅ Form 2: Trustee Information Form

✅ Form 3: Financial Information Form (if Treasurer)

✅ Form 4: Disqualification Check Declaration

✅ DBS/Background Check (if working with vulnerable groups)

✅ Sign Constitution acknowledgment

✅ Complete induction briefing

✅ Add to trustee contact list

✅ Arrange first trustee meeting attendance

---

**All forms must be retained securely for 7 years as per Charity Commission requirements.**
